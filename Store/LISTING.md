## Description
Iridia plays GameCube games you already own on iPhone and iPad.

Fast without JIT
Iridia is tuned for Apple silicon: an optimised interpreter, Metal graphics and profile-guided
builds. Pick up to 8x internal resolution on recent iPhones, and Iridia automatically keeps your
phone cool when it gets warm.

Made for iPhone
• A glass shelf for your library, with cover art
• Pick up exactly where you left off, or start at the title screen to load your in-game save
• Precise touch controls, or any MFi, Xbox, PlayStation or Switch controller with smart mapping
• Save states, memory cards, per-game settings
• Live performance panel: resolution, frame rate and CPU clock

Free to try
Every game launch is a free play session of 10 minutes of play, as many as you like. A one-time
"Unlock Full Version" purchase removes the limit. No subscription, no ads, no tracking.

Bring your own games
Iridia does not include any games, BIOS or firmware. Add your own legally obtained game files
(disc backups or homebrew) with the Files app. ISO, RVZ, GCM and CISO files are supported, and ZIP
and 7Z archives are unpacked automatically.

Open source
Iridia is free software under the GNU GPL, based on the Dolphin emulator. Source code: [URL].
