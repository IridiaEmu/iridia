# Iridia Privacy Policy

_Last updated: [DATE]_

Iridia does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

**On your device only.** Your games, saves, settings and screenshots stay on your device (and in
your own iCloud or device backups, if you use them).

**Network requests you can choose to make:**
- **Cover art:** when cover downloads are on, Iridia asks GameTDB (gametdb.com) for a game's cover
  using the game's ID. GameTDB may see your IP address, as with any web request. Turn this off in
  Settings.
- **Your own game sources:** if you add a remote source (for example a WebDAV server), Iridia
  connects to the address you entered.
- **Wi-Fi upload and handing a game to another device:** these use your local network only, and only
  while you use them.

**Purchases** are processed by Apple. Iridia receives only Apple's confirmation that the
purchase is valid; it never sees your payment details.

**Children.** Iridia collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes.** If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here.
