Drafts for the Wii U update. Nothing here has been sent anywhere.

## App Review notes (App Store Connect, "Notes" for the reviewer)

```
This update adds experimental Wii U support next to the existing GameCube support.

The app contains no games, no console keys and no firmware, and it does not link to
any. Like the GameCube side, players use their own game files, which they copy into
the app's folder with the Files app (On My iPhone > Iridia > Wii U). All emulation
runs as an interpreter: the app does not generate or execute code at runtime (no
JIT). Online features are turned off.

How to see the Wii U section and the purchase without a game:
1. Open the app. On the library screen (the shelf, also when it is empty), tap
   "Wii U" in the bottom tab bar.
2. The Wii U (Experimental) screen opens. Tap "Unlock" at the top right to open the
   purchase screen (Unlock Full Version, Restore Purchases).
3. The same purchase screen is also in Settings (bottom tab bar) > Full Version.
4. Tap "GameCube" in the bottom tab bar to go back.

Purchase: the existing one-time In-App Purchase "Unlock Full Version"
(iridia.unlock.full) now covers both GameCube and Wii U. Without it, each Wii U game
plays in free play sessions of 20 minutes (GameCube stays at 10 minutes); the
sessions repeat without limit. When a session ends, the game pauses and the
purchase screen appears. Restore Purchases is on the purchase screen and in the Wii
U Settings.

Wii U games need a device with 8 GB of memory (iPhone 15 Pro or later, iPad with
M1 or later, iPad mini with A17 Pro). On other devices the Wii U section still
opens and explains this, and the purchase screen is still reachable.
```

## Update notes ("What's New in This Version")

```
Experimental Wii U support

- A new Wii U section, next to your GameCube library. Tap Wii U in the tab bar.
- Add your own Wii U games and keys with the Files app: On My iPhone > Iridia > Wii U.
- Touch controls laid out like the GamePad, game controller support, and an
  optional second screen for GamePad games.
- Choose how the TV and GamePad screens are shown, swap them any time, and draw
  on the GamePad screen with your finger or Apple Pencil.
- Your Unlock Full Version purchase now covers Wii U too. Without it, Wii U games
  play in free sessions of 20 minutes.

Wii U support is experimental: it needs a device with 8 GB of memory, and games
may run slowly or show glitches. Iridia includes no games, keys or firmware.
```
