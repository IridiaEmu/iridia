Iridia Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Iridia does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no crash reporting.

On your device only

Your games, saves, settings and screenshots stay on your device, and in your own iCloud or device backups if you use them.

Network requests you can choose to make

  • Cover art: when cover downloads are on, Iridia asks GameTDB (gametdb.com) for a game's cover using the game's ID. GameTDB may see your IP address, as with any web request. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • Your own game sources: if you add a remote source (for example a WebDAV server), Iridia connects to the address you entered.
  • Wi-Fi upload and sending a game to another device: these use your local network only, and only while you use them.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. Iridia only receives Apple's confirmation that a purchase is valid. It never sees your payment details.

Children

Iridia collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
