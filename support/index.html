Iridia Support

Help for Iridia on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email iridiadev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and the game you were playing. You can also use Report a Bug in Iridia's Settings, which fills in your device and app version for you.

Which iPhones work?

Iridia needs an iPhone 12 or newer. How well a game runs depends on the game and on your phone. Every time you start a game you get 10 minutes of free play, so you can check how your games run before you buy the unlock.

How do I add games?

Iridia doesn't include any games. Add your own game files (your disc backups or homebrew):

  • In Iridia, tap Choose a Game File, or
  • In the Files app, copy them to On My iPhone → Iridia → Games.

ISO, RVZ, GCM and CISO files work. ZIP and 7Z archives are unpacked for you.

I bought the unlock on another device

Open the unlock screen (tap Unlock in your library) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

A game runs slowly or gets hot

Try a lower resolution in the in-game Performance panel. Phones slow down when they get warm, so a game can run faster at the start of a session than after 20 minutes. Taking the case off or playing somewhere cooler helps.
