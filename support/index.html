Iridia Support

Help for Iridia on iPhone and iPad.

Contact

Email iridiadev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone or iPad model, your iOS version, and the game you were playing. You can also use Report a Bug in Iridia's Settings, which fills in your device and app version for you.

Which iPhones work?

Iridia needs iOS 26 or later. GameCube plays best on newer phones, and Wii games run best on recent iPhones and iPads. Wii U needs a device with 8 GB of memory: iPhone 15 Pro or later, an iPad with M1 or later, or iPad mini with A17 Pro. How well a game runs depends on the game and on your device. Every time you start a game you get free play time (10 minutes for GameCube and Wii, 20 for Wii U), so you can check how your games run before you buy the unlock.

How do I add games?

Iridia includes no games, keys, BIOS or firmware. Add your own game files (your disc backups or homebrew):

  • In Iridia, tap +, then Choose a Game File, or
  • In the Files app, copy them to On My iPhone → Iridia, into the GameCube, Wii or Wii U folder. (The GameCube folder was named Games before version 1.2.)

GameCube: ISO, RVZ, GCM and CISO. Wii: RVZ, WIA, ISO, WBFS, GCZ and CISO, plus homebrew; RVZ is the best choice. Wii U: WUA files, title folders and homebrew (RPX). ZIP and 7Z files are unpacked for you.

I bought the unlock on another device

Open the unlock screen (tap Unlock in your library) and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple. To ask for a refund, go to reportaproblem.apple.com.

A game runs slowly or gets hot

Try a lower resolution in the in-game Performance panel. Phones slow down when they get warm, so a game can run faster at the start of a session than after 20 minutes. Taking the case off or playing somewhere cooler helps.
